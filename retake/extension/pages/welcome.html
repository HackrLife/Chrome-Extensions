Set up in one minute

Microphone and camera

So you can talk over your demo and show the camera bubble. Chrome asks once; Retake only uses them while you record.

Keep your tools on screen when you change pages (optional)

Without this, the drawing tools and control bar disappear when the recorded tab loads a new page; you can bring them back from the Retake icon. Chrome describes this as reading and changing data on websites. Retake never reads page content or sends anything anywhere.

Pin Retake to your toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Retake.

Keyboard shortcuts

Pause or resumeAlt + Shift + K
Retake the last partAlt + Shift + U
Drawing tools on or offAlt + Shift + D
Finish and open the editorAlt + Shift + S

On a Mac, Alt is the Option key. Change shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
