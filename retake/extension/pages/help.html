Common questions

Where are my recordings stored?

On this computer, inside Chrome’s storage for Retake. Open My recordings to find, rename or delete them. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

Clearing Chrome’s browsing data for extensions, or removing Retake, deletes them. Use Back up all in My recordings to save a copy.

The microphone or camera doesn’t work

Open the welcome page and press Allow. If Chrome says they are blocked, click the icon at the left of the address bar on that page and allow Microphone and Camera.

On a Mac, also check System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and Camera, and make sure Google Chrome is switched on.

Why can’t I record or draw on some pages?

Chrome doesn’t let any extension record or draw on its own pages (addresses starting with chrome://), the Chrome Web Store, or new-tab pages. Open a website, or choose Window or Full screen to record those.

Why is there no system sound when I record a window or screen?

When recording a tab, Retake captures the tab’s sound. For a full screen, Chrome offers Share system audio on Windows and ChromeOS; macOS does not allow it. Recording a window never includes its sound.

How does Retake work?

Every time you pause, Retake starts a new part of the recording. Retake throws away the last part and records it again, so earlier footage is never touched. Press Alt + Shift + U to do it from the keyboard.

Does the control bar appear in my video?

When recording a tab, the bar is part of the page, so it can appear. It fades away a couple of seconds after you move the pointer elsewhere, and returns when you move back towards it. Keyboard shortcuts let you pause, retake and finish without touching it.

Why does export take as long as the video?

Retake builds the final video on your computer by playing your recording once, with trims and callouts applied. Keep the editor tab open until it finishes. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I present slides with my camera?

Open Retake, choose Slides and press Start recording. Add a PDF (each page becomes a slide), images or short videos, then press Start presenting. Your slides fill the screen with your camera in a corner; change slides with the arrow keys or a clicker, press D to draw and Esc to stop. The recording is exactly what you see, without the controls or the script. Your files stay on this computer.

Is Retake free?

Yes. Recording, editing and exporting are free, with no watermark and no time limit.

Report a problem or suggest a feature

Report it on GitHub

The report opens already filled in with your Retake and Chrome versions, your system and Retake’s recent error messages, so you only need to say what happened. You can read and change everything before you send it. Retake collects no data, so this is the only way we hear about problems.
