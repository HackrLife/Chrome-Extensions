# Retake privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Retake is a screen recorder that runs on your computer, as a Chrome extension, a web app and a desktop app. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: your recordings never leave your computer. The Chrome extension collects no data at all, and the web app keeps only your email address.

## What Retake stores

- **Your recordings, edits and settings** are stored on your computer, in Chrome's storage for the Retake extension. They never leave your computer unless you export or back them up yourself.
- **Exports, screenshots and backups** are saved to your Downloads folder, where you choose what to do with them.

## Your account (web app)

The web app asks you to sign up with your email address before you record. We keep:

- **Your email address**, so you can sign in with an emailed link and so we know who uses Retake.
- **Your email choice.** We send you tips, new features and offers from Retake and other HackrLife tools only if you tick the box to say yes. You can unsubscribe from any of those emails at any time.

Sign-in emails are sent for you to sign in and are not marketing. Your account is held in our HackrLife apps account system and is never sold. To delete your account, open an issue at https://github.com/HackrLife/chrome-extensions/issues or email dev@hackrlife.com.

## What Retake does not do

- The Chrome extension and desktop app have no account and no sign-in.
- It does not upload recordings or any other data to any server, apart from the web app's sign-in.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising, and it contains no third-party code that does.
- It does not read the content of the web pages you visit. The tools shown on a page only draw on top of it.
- The key display shows keyboard shortcuts such as Ctrl + C. It never shows ordinary typing, and it is switched off in password fields.

## Microphone, camera and screen

Retake uses your microphone, camera and screen only while you are recording or presenting, and only after you have allowed it in Chrome. You can withdraw these permissions at any time in Chrome's settings.

## Deleting your data

Delete recordings in **My recordings**. Removing the Retake extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/HackrLife/chrome-extensions/issues.
