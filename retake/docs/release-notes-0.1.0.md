# Retake 0.1.0: release notes

First public release of the Retake Chrome extension. Free, with no account, no upload and no watermark.

## What it does

- **Record** a tab, a window, your whole screen, just your camera or just your voice, with your microphone and the tab's sound.
- **Read while you record.** The teleprompter scrolls in a small window, stops when you pause and goes back when you retake. It is never in a tab recording.
- **Retake** the last part with Alt + Shift + U. Everything before it stays as it was.
- **Draw on the page** while you record: pen, highlighter, arrows, boxes, circles, text and numbered steps, fading after 3, 5 or 10 seconds if you like. Spotlight, click pulse and keyboard display.
- **Edit afterwards:** trim, then add arrows, boxes, steps, text, zooms and blur on a timeline.
- **Export** MP4, WebM, GIF or audio, in landscape or vertical 9:16, straight to your Downloads.
- **My recordings** keeps everything on your computer, with backup and restore, and recovers recordings interrupted by a crash.
- **Present only** mode: the drawing tools and spotlight on a live call, with nothing recorded.

## Good to know

- Window and screen recordings open a small Retake window, which records and stays minimised until you finish. Keep it open.
- The control bar is part of the page in a tab recording. It hides while you record; use the keyboard shortcuts to avoid showing it at all.
- Report a problem from Help. The report opens on GitHub, filled in with your versions and Retake's recent error messages, and nothing is sent unless you submit it.

## Shortcuts

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Pause or resume | Alt + Shift + K |
| Retake the last part | Alt + Shift + U |
| Drawing tools on or off | Alt + Shift + D |
| Finish | Alt + Shift + S |

Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
